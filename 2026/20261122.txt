In 1995 a 21-year-old from Paris wrote Iggy Pop twenty pages about her parents' divorce. He wrote back by hand, and began: "I read the whole [...] thing, dear." [The story](https://open.substack.com/pub/infiniteloops/p/osv-field-notes-42) is told as a lament for letters: in a letter, people say what they'd say to one person only.

The paper isn't what was lost. What she got was attention, read to the end by someone who owed her nothing. A message can carry that; a letter can skip it. [Be quick to listen, slow to speak](https://thesouldevotional.github.io/soul/post.html?p=bible/james_1-19.txt#v19). The listening comes first, because the other person can tell whether you did it.

!!Before any answer, someone needs to know you read the whole thing.!!
