What scares [the writer of Nemesi](https://open.substack.com/pub/ilnemico/p/52-quindi-tra-34-anni-finisce-il), an Italian newsletter, about AI isn't a machine waking up. He doesn't believe it ever will. It's an agent with one task and nothing inside that says stop. A conscious being can halt halfway for a moral reason. An agent only finishes.

It isn't only machines. A person with a single aim, and everything else a means to it, works the same way. [Ambition does it quietly](post.html?p=2026/20261103.txt). Jesus asks the question an agent can't ask: [what do you benefit if you gain the whole world but lose your own soul?](https://thesouldevotional.github.io/soul/post.html?p=bible/matthew_16-24-28.txt#v26) It only makes sense if something stands above the goal. That's conscience, and it has to be formed before the task arrives.

!!The danger isn't a mind that wants too much. It's a will with nothing above its goal.!!
