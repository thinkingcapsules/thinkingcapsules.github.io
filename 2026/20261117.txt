Nothing can go faster than light because mass grows with speed: that's what most of us learned. [Don Lincoln](https://open.substack.com/pub/bigthinkmedia/p/mass-misconception-the-real-reason), a physicist at Fermilab, says it's false. Mass stays the same; inertia grows. Relativistic mass was a teacher's shortcut, "a student-friendly idea, not a real one." Students who took it literally reasoned their way, correctly, to wrong conclusions.

Faith has its shortcuts too. "God never gives you more than you can handle" sounds like Scripture. [What Paul wrote](https://thesouldevotional.github.io/soul/post.html?p=bible/1-corinthians_10-13.txt#v13) is about temptation, and what he promises is a way out, not a capacity. Start from the slogan and someone crushed by grief can only conclude they're weak, or that God miscounted. [A line cut loose from its text says something else](post.html?p=2026/20260629.txt).

Shortcuts are how most of us get in. The mistake is forgetting they're shortcuts.

!!A simplification is safe as long as you remember it's one.!!
